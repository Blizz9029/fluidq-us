How this maps to your NSE screen. Every calculation is unchanged: absolute return over N months is the last close against the last close on or before the calendar lookback date; the ranking factor is the plain average of the 12-month and 6-month absolute returns; median daily volume is the median of close × volume over the last 252 sessions; percentage of positive days is the share of sessions with a positive close-to-close move. Prices are split-adjusted and dividend-unadjusted, so returns are price returns, matching NSE convention. Rupee-denominated thresholds were restated in dollars — volume, market cap and price — because the units differ; the formulas behind them did not change. The Series EQ/BE toggle has no US counterpart and is dropped.

Data: Yahoo Finance daily bars and quotes, refreshed after each US close. Beta is against SPY. For research and screening only — not investment advice, and not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
